# Malay Language Adventure 🌟
### Selamat Datang! Welcome to Your Malay Learning Journey

An interactive, AI-powered web application designed to help Singapore primary school students master Malay as a second language. Whether you're preparing for PSLE or simply want to improve your Malay proficiency, this platform makes learning engaging, personalized, and effective.

---

## 🎯 What is Malay Language Adventure?

Malay Language Adventure is a comprehensive learning platform that combines traditional language education with modern AI technology. It provides an immersive, gamified experience that adapts to each student's learning pace and progress.

### Who is this for?

- **Primary School Students** (P1-P6) learning Malay as a second language
- **Secondary 1 Students** looking to strengthen their foundation
- **Non-native Malay speakers** who want structured, progressive learning
- **Teachers and Parents** seeking supplementary learning resources
- **Anyone passionate** about mastering the Malay language

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📚 **Four Core Learning Modules**

1. **Imbuhan (Affixes)** - Master prefixes, suffixes, and circumfixes
   - meN-, beR-, peN-, di-, ter-, ke-
   - Word formation and transformation rules
   - Comprehensive nasal assimilation patterns

2. **Peribahasa (Proverbs & Idioms)** - Learn Malay wisdom and expressions
   - 80+ commonly used proverbs
   - Contextual usage examples
   - Cultural insights and meanings

3. **Penjodoh Bilangan (Numerical Classifiers)** - Proper quantifier usage
   - Classifiers for people, animals, objects
   - Food, places, and abstract concepts
   - Correct grammatical application

4. **Kosa Kata (Vocabulary)** - Expand your word bank
   - Thematic vocabulary groups
   - Synonyms and antonyms
   - Daily word practice

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Learning**

- **Adaptive Quiz Generation** - Google Gemini AI creates personalized questions based on your skill level
- **Daily Vocabulary** - 5 new words delivered fresh every day with definitions and examples
- **Progressive Difficulty** - Questions automatically adjust as you improve
- **Contextual Learning** - Questions designed around real-world scenarios

### 🎮 **Gamified Progress System**

Track your journey through four mastery tiers:
1. **Murid Baru** (New Student) - Just starting out
2. **Pencari Ilmu** (Knowledge Seeker) - Building foundations
3. **Pakar Muda** (Young Expert) - Gaining confidence
4. **Mahaguru** (Master Teacher) - Achieving mastery

### 👤 **Personalized User Profiles**

- Individual progress tracking
- Score accumulation and achievement system
- Persistent learning history
- Customized difficulty adjustments

## 🚀 Quick Start

### For Students

1. **Open the Application** - Load `learnmalay.html` in your web browser
2. **Create Your Profile** - Register with a unique username
3. **Start Learning** - Choose a module and begin your quiz journey
4. **Practice Daily** - Check your daily vocabulary and complete exercises
5. **Track Progress** - Watch your tier advancement as you learn

### For Teachers & Parents

This platform can be used to:
- Supplement classroom learning
- Provide homework assignments
- Track student progress and weaknesses
- Identify areas needing extra attention
- Prepare students for PSLE examinations

## 🎓 Educational Foundation

This application is aligned with Singapore's Primary Malay Language syllabus and PSLE examination requirements. The learning content covers:

- **Vocabulary Building** (Perbendaharaan Kata)
- **Grammar Fundamentals** (Tatabahasa)
- **Affixes & Word Formation** (Imbuhan)
- **Reading Comprehension** (Kefahaman Membaca)
- **Oral Communication** (Lisan)
- **Listening Skills** (Mendengar)
- **Writing & Composition** (Penulisan Karangan)

For a deep dive into Malay language learning content, see our **[Comprehensive Learning Guide](./LEARNING_GUIDE.md)**.

## 🌟 Learning Tips

### For Best Results:
1. **Practice Daily** - Even 15 minutes a day builds strong habits
2. **Review Mistakes** - Learn from incorrect answers
3. **Use Multiple Modules** - Balanced practice across all areas
4. **Speak Out Loud** - Practice pronunciation while learning
5. **Apply in Real Life** - Use new words and phrases daily
6. **Don't Rush** - Quality learning beats speed
7. **Track Your Progress** - Celebrate small victories

### Recommended Study Routine:
- **Morning**: Review daily vocabulary (5-10 minutes)
- **Afternoon**: Complete 1-2 module quizzes (15-20 minutes)
- **Evening**: Practice writing or speaking (10-15 minutes)
- **Weekly**: Review all mistakes and challenging concepts
