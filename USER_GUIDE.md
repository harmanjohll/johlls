## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- **Web Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
- **Internet Connection:** Required for AI quiz generation and user data sync
- **Device:** Desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone
- **Screen Size:** Optimized for all screen sizes (responsive design)

### First Time Setup

1. **Open the Application**
   - Navigate to the `learnmalay.html` file
   - Double-click to open in your default browser
   - OR right-click and choose "Open with" your preferred browser

2. **Create Your Account**
   - Enter a unique username (e.g., "Ali123", "Siti_P6")
   - Click **"Daftar (New User)"** button
   - Your profile will be created instantly

3. **Start Learning!**
   - You'll see your dashboard with 4 learning modules
   - Daily vocabulary will load automatically
   - Choose any module to begin

## For Students

### Your Dashboard

When you log in, you'll see:

#### 🌅 Daily Vocabulary Section
- **5 new words** updated every day at midnight
- Each word includes:
  - Malay word
  - English translation
  - Example sentence in Malay
- **Tip:** Review these every morning before school!

#### 📚 Four Learning Modules

1. **Imbuhan (Affixes)**
   - Learn prefixes: meN-, ber-, di-, ter-, peN-, ke-
   - Practice word formation
   - Master nasal assimilation rules

2. **Peribahasa (Proverbs)**
   - 80+ Malay idioms and sayings
   - Learn meanings and usage
   - Practice in context

3. **Penjodoh Bilangan (Classifiers)**
   - Correct usage of orang, ekor, buah, biji, etc.
   - Categorized by object types
   - Real-world application

4. **Kosa Kata (Vocabulary)**
   - General vocabulary building
   - Thematic word groups
   - Synonyms and antonyms

### How to Take a Quiz

1. **Click on a module** card (e.g., "Imbuhan")
2. **Wait for questions to generate** (AI-powered, takes 5-10 seconds)
3. **Answer 10 multiple-choice questions**
   - Read each question carefully
   - Consider all options
   - Click your answer
4. **Get instant feedback**
   - Correct answer = Green highlight + points
   - Wrong answer = Red highlight + see correct answer
5. **View your score** at the end
6. **Return to dashboard** to try another module

### Progress Tracking

#### Your Tier System

You advance through four tiers based on accumulated points:

| Tier | Name | Points Required |
|------|------|-----------------|
| 🌱 | **Murid Baru** (New Student) | 0-99 |
| 📖 | **Pencari Ilmu** (Knowledge Seeker) | 100-299 |
| 🎓 | **Pakar Muda** (Young Expert) | 300-599 |
| 👑 | **Mahaguru** (Master Teacher) | 600+ |

#### Scoring System

- **Each correct answer:** +10 points
- **Each incorrect answer:** +0 points
- **Module completion:** Bonus satisfaction!

**Tip:** Complete all 4 modules regularly for balanced learning.

### Study Tips for Students

#### Daily Routine (15-30 minutes)

**Morning (5-10 min):**
- Review daily vocabulary
- Read each word aloud
- Try using one word in a sentence

**Afternoon (15-20 min):**
- Complete 1-2 module quizzes
- Focus on modules you find challenging
- Review wrong answers

**Evening (Optional):**
- Practice writing sentences with new words
- Speak Malay with family

#### Exam Preparation

**1 Month Before PSLE:**
- Complete 2-3 quizzes daily
- Focus on Imbuhan and Peribahasa (heavily tested)
- Review all wrong answers from previous quizzes

**1 Week Before:**
- Quick daily reviews
- Don't stress - trust your preparation
- Get good sleep

#### Learning Strategies

1. **Learn from Mistakes**
   - When you get an answer wrong, read the correct answer carefully
   - Understand WHY it's correct
   - Try to remember the rule or pattern

2. **Pattern Recognition**
   - Notice patterns in imbuhan rules
   - Common proverbs for similar situations
   - Classifier categories

3. **Active Practice**
   - Don't just read - say words aloud
   - Write example sentences
   - Teach concepts to someone else

4. **Consistent Over Intensive**
   - 15 minutes daily > 2 hours once a week
   - Build habits, not cramming sessions

## For Teachers

### Classroom Integration

#### As Homework
- Assign specific modules as homework
- Ask students to complete 2 quizzes per module per week
- Track progress by asking about tier levels

#### As Warm-Up Activity
- Start class with daily vocabulary review
- Students take 1 quick quiz (5 minutes)
- Discuss challenging questions as a class

#### As Assessment Preparation
- Use platform to supplement PSLE preparation
- Focus students on modules matching upcoming test topics
- Review common mistakes together

### Monitoring Student Progress

#### What to Ask Students:
- "What tier are you at now?"
- "Which module do you find most challenging?"
- "What were your common mistakes?"
- "Did you learn any new peribahasa this week?"

#### Encouraging Usage:
- Set weekly goals (e.g., "Reach 50 points this week")
- Create friendly competitions (who can reach Pakar Muda first?)
- Celebrate tier advancements in class
- Give bonus points for consistent platform usage

### Teaching Tips Using the Platform

1. **Supplement, Don't Replace**
   - Use alongside textbook lessons
   - Platform reinforces what you teach
   - Not a substitute for direct instruction

2. **Address Common Errors**
   - Ask students about questions they got wrong
   - Use those as teaching moments
   - Explain difficult concepts in class

3. **Differentiated Learning**
   - Advanced students: Challenge them to reach Mahaguru tier
   - Struggling students: Focus on one module at a time
   - Platform adapts difficulty based on performance

### Classroom Activities Ideas

**Activity 1: Peribahasa Showdown**
- Students complete Peribahasa module
- Hold class quiz competition using the same idioms
- Winner gets to teach one proverb to the class

**Activity 2: Imbuhan Detective**
- Students take Imbuhan quiz
- Discuss the trickiest questions together
- Break down the nasal assimilation rules on whiteboard

**Activity 3: Daily Word Challenge**
- One student shares their daily words
- Class creates sentences using those words
- Vote for most creative sentence

## For Parents

### Supporting Your Child's Learning

#### Even If You Don't Speak Malay

You can still help your child succeed!

**What You Can Do:**

1. **Encourage Consistency**
   - Remind child to use platform daily
   - Set aside dedicated "Malay time"
   - Make it part of evening routine

2. **Monitor Progress**
   - Ask: "What tier are you at?"
   - Ask: "How many quizzes did you complete today?"
   - Celebrate when they advance tiers

3. **Create Learning Space**
   - Quiet area free from distractions
   - Good lighting
   - Device with stable internet

4. **Provide Positive Reinforcement**
   - Praise effort, not just results
   - "I'm proud you practiced even though it's hard"
   - Small rewards for tier advancements

#### If You Speak Malay

**Engage More Deeply:**

1. **Practice Together**
   - Review daily vocabulary with them
   - Quiz them on peribahasa meanings
   - Have simple Malay conversations at home

2. **Contextualize Learning**
   - Show how peribahasa are used in real life
   - Point out imbuhan patterns in newspapers
   - Use vocabulary in daily situations

3. **Teach Cultural Context**
   - Explain proverb origins and wisdom
   - Share your own language learning journey
   - Connect language to Malay culture and values

### What to Expect

#### First Week
- Child exploring different modules
- Learning interface and how quizzes work
- May make many mistakes (this is normal!)

#### After 2-4 Weeks
- Should be in Pencari Ilmu tier (100+ points)
- More confident with interface
- Starting to recognize patterns
- Fewer mistakes on repeated concepts

#### After 2-3 Months
- Approaching or reaching Pakar Muda tier (300+ points)
- Vocabulary noticeably expanding
- Using Malay phrases more naturally
- Better exam performance

### Red Flags to Watch For

🚩 **Child never wants to use platform**
- May indicate difficulty level too high
- Suggest focusing on one easier module
- Check if they need additional tutoring

🚩 **Always getting same questions wrong**
- Indicates gap in understanding
- Recommend teacher consultation
- May need concept re-teaching

🚩 **Rushing through without reading**
- Not actually learning, just clicking
- Monitor quiz sessions
- Discuss importance of careful reading

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "Questions are not loading"
**Possible causes:**
- No internet connection
- API quota exceeded (rare)
- Browser compatibility

**Solutions:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page (F5)
- Try a different browser
- Wait a few minutes and try again

#### "I forgot my username"
**Solution:**
- Unfortunately, there's no password recovery since no email is stored
- You'll need to create a new account
- **Prevention:** Write down your username somewhere safe

#### "My score disappeared"
**Possible causes:**
- Browser cache cleared
- Using a different device
- Database sync issue

**Solutions:**
- Always use the same device if possible
- Don't clear browser data frequently
- Your score is stored in the cloud, so logging in again should retrieve it

#### "Daily vocabulary not updating"
**Solutions:**
- Clear your browser cache
- Wait until after midnight for new words
- Refresh the page (F5)

#### "Quiz questions are too difficult"
**Solutions:**
- Start with Kosa Kata module (easiest)
- Review the LEARNING_GUIDE.md for concept explanations
- Practice more frequently - AI will adjust difficulty
- Ask teacher for help with concepts

#### "The app looks broken on my phone"
**Solutions:**
- The app is responsive but works best on larger screens
- Use landscape mode on phones
- If possible, use a tablet or computer
- Update your mobile browser

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is this free to use?**
A: Yes! The platform is educational and free for all students.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: Yes, a simple username-only account helps track your progress.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Yes, but the experience is better on tablets or computers.

**Q: How long should I use this daily?**
A: Recommended 15-30 minutes per day for best results.

### Learning Questions

**Q: Which module should I start with?**
A: Start with whichever topic you're learning in school, or try Kosa Kata (vocabulary) as it's the most accessible.

**Q: How do I know if I'm improving?**
A: Track your tier advancement and notice if you're making fewer mistakes on repeated topics.

**Q: What if I keep getting questions wrong?**
A: Review the LEARNING_GUIDE.md for concept explanations, and ask your teacher for help. Don't give up!

**Q: Can I retake quizzes?**
A: Yes! Every quiz is AI-generated and different each time.

**Q: Will this help me pass PSLE?**
A: This platform supplements your school learning and PSLE preparation. Use it alongside textbooks and teacher instruction for best results.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why do questions take time to load?**
A: The AI generates unique questions for you in real-time. This usually takes 5-10 seconds.

**Q: Can I use this offline?**
A: No, internet connection is required for AI question generation and score syncing.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. We only store your username and learning progress. No personal information required.

**Q: Can I use this on multiple devices?**
A: Yes, your progress syncs across devices when you log in with the same username.
